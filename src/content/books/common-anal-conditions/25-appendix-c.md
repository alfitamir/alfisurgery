# APPENDIX C — WHEN NOT TO DO A DRE OR RECTAL INSTRUMENTATION

**Rectal instrumentation** includes DRE, anoscopy, proctoscopy, enemas, suppositories, rectal thermometers, rectal tubes, and faecal management systems. **None of these rules may delay urgent surgical review.** [C][L]

| ☐   | Situation                                                                           | Why                                  | Safer alternative                                                                                                                |
| --- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ☐   | Profound neutropenia (ANC < 0.5 × 10⁹/L or per local protocol)                      | Bacteraemia, perianal sepsis         | External inspection and gentle palpation; CT or MRI; antibiotics within 1 h; early surgical review; senior DRE only if essential |
| ☐   | Severe thrombocytopenia or coagulopathy (threshold per local protocol)              | Bleeding                             | External inspection; correct coagulopathy if instrumentation is essential; specialist decision                                   |
| ☐   | Acute anal fissure with severe spasm                                                | Intense pain; unreliable examination | Treat spasm; re-examine in 2–3 weeks; EUA if red flags                                                                           |
| ☐   | Suspected necrotising infection or haemodynamic instability                         | Delays source control                | Call the surgeon; resuscitate; examination in theatre                                                                            |
| ☐   | Rectal foreign body that may be sharp or broken, or with perforation risk           | Injury to patient and examiner       | Imaging first; removal under anaesthesia                                                                                         |
| ☐   | Recent rectal or anal surgery (anastomosis, repair)                                 | Disruption of repair                 | Ask the operating team before any instrumentation                                                                                |
| ☐   | Anticoagulation where instrumentation (rather than gentle DRE) could cause bleeding | Bleeding                             | Defer biopsy or procedure until a plan is agreed with the prescriber                                                             |
| ☐   | Child with possible sexual abuse                                                    | Re-traumatisation; loss of evidence  | Specialist paediatric forensic examination via the safeguarding pathway                                                          |
| ☐   | Refusal or lack of capacity                                                         | Consent                              | Respect refusal; capacity and best-interest pathway; EUA where justified                                                         |

<small>Pregnancy is not a contraindication to a gentle, indicated examination (use left lateral or left-tilted position after 20 weeks).</small>
