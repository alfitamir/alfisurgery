## Why This Handbook Exists — The MDH Audit Reality

Between 2020 and 2025, our department at [Mohammad Dossary Hospital](https://mdh.com.sa/) audited **987 anorectal procedure records** (**845 LigaSure excisions [85.6%]**, **106 stapled procedures [10.7%]**, and **36 conventional or other procedures [3.6%]**, alongside **336 sphincter procedures**, **167 fissurectomies**, and **25 fistulectomies or fistulotomies**). Across the Emergency Department, primary-care clinics, and inpatient wards, one lesson stood out:

- **The “just haemorrhoids” reflex is the most dangerous reflex in anorectal care.**

- Most avoidable anorectal harm — delayed cancer diagnosis, missed abscess, iatrogenic sphincter injury — happens **before** the patient reaches a surgeon.

- This handbook gives emergency physicians, family physicians, medical specialists, and nurses a fast, colour-coded, algorithmic system to get the diagnosis, the first treatment, and the escalation right the first time.
